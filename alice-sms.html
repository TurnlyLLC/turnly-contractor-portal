Turnly A.L.I.C.E. private assistant

This optional text service is for Turnly's authorized owner only. It provides operational business updates, answers to account questions, and confirmations of requested administrative actions. It sends no marketing messages.

Text-message consent

The authorized owner may opt in by texting START to (252) 542-5686. By sending START, you agree to receive A.L.I.C.E. operational SMS replies and requested business updates from Turnly LLC. Message frequency varies, up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or use of the Turnly portal.

Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. You may opt back in with START. Only the registered owner phone can issue commands. Do not text passwords, bank account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive identity information.
