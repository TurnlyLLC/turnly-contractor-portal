Turnly Professional Cleaners Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Turnly Professional Cleaners ("Turnly," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the Turnly portal, cleaning operations, account access, turn requests, service scheduling, quality assurance, and related SMS notifications.

View Terms and Conditions Portal Home

Information We Collect

We may collect account and business contact information, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, company or property association, role, login information, and communication preferences.

We may also collect operational information needed to provide cleaning services, including property names, unit numbers, scheduled move-in dates, turn request details, service status, feedback, checklist responses, photos, videos, and messages exchanged through the portal.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide, manage, and improve Turnly services, including account access, scheduling, contractor assignment, property manager communication, turn request updates, QA review, service documentation, billing support, and customer support.

We may use mobile phone numbers to send operational SMS notifications, such as new turn request alerts, schedule updates, account notices, and support-related messages.

SMS and Mobile Information

By opting in to Turnly SMS notifications, you agree to receive operational text messages from Turnly Professional Cleaners. Message frequency varies based on portal activity and may be up to 100 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request assistance by replying HELP or contacting us at ops@turnlypros.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not share, sell, rent, release, or transfer mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in records, or text messaging originator opt-in data and consent to any third party, affiliate, or other organization for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS opt-in consent is collected only for Turnly Professional Cleaners operational notifications and is not transferable to another business, affiliate, or marketing partner.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate our business and provide services, such as with technology providers, messaging providers, hosting providers, payment or accounting providers, customer support tools, or contractors who need information to perform assigned work.

Service providers are authorized to use information only as needed to provide services to Turnly and may not use Turnly information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

All categories of information sharing described in this Privacy Policy exclude SMS opt-in records and text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. That messaging consent information will not be shared with any third party or affiliate. Mobile phone numbers and messaging consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain operational records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. We may retain service records, QA media, messages, and scheduling history for business and quality-control purposes.

Your Choices

You may request updates to your account information or ask questions about this Privacy Policy by contacting ops@turnlypros.com. You may opt out of SMS notifications by replying STOP.
