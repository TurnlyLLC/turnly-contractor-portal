Your daily routine

  1. Review Overview: check overdue follow-ups and upcoming walkthroughs.
  2. Open Prospects: filter by owner or stage, then use the qualification workspace to work your list.
  3. Keep Contacts current: open the property record and update its decision maker, phone, email, and notes.
  4. Set the next action: record the outcome, assign ownership, and schedule a follow-up.
  5. Move the opportunity: update its stage on Deal pipeline after a real milestone.
  6. Prepare the handoff: capture pricing fit and scope, schedule a walkthrough, and send the opportunity to management review.

Pipeline definitions

New → Contacted → Pricing confirmed → Walkthrough set → Management review → Won / Lost

Pricing confirmed means the prospect has accepted the discussed pricing fit. Won means management has confirmed the agreement. Keep the stage aligned with what actually happened.

Proposals and closing

Review scope, pricing, recipient details, and the generated PDF before sharing a proposal. Email tools prepare a draft; review it before sending. Track the agreed status in Closing.

Split leads between operators

In Prospects, select leads and choose Assign / split leads. Select one operator for a single owner or multiple operators for an even split of the selected leads. Only unassigned leads are included by default; uncheck that option to reassign existing owners. Use My leads, Unassigned, or an operator filter to work a list. Assignment history records who made each change.

Residential workspace

This CRM displays residential leads only. Commercial records and their notes remain in the database and the existing admin sales tools. Residential website inquiries arrive here automatically. New and imported CRM leads are residential and initially unassigned.

Permissions and team handoff

Administrators create and import prospects and manage staff access. Sales staff work existing opportunities, qualify prospects, maintain records, and track follow-ups. Team shows ownership and workload; ask your administrator to add or disable a staff account.

Agent partners is the residential referral workspace. Its flyers and referral activity stay connected to the existing partner records.

Keep the record useful

Use factual notes, a clear owner, and a dated next step. Search before requesting a new prospect to avoid duplicates. Only mark outcomes that have occurred.
