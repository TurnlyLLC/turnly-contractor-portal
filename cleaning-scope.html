A few things to know before booking

Standard cleaning — full scope & service guidelines

STANDARD RESIDENTIAL CLEANING

An everyday reset for a well-maintained home.

Know what to expect, room by room. Cleaning is limited to safely accessible areas; heavy furniture and appliances are not moved.

IncludedNot includedAdd-onsService guidelines

What’s included

Kitchen

Our cleaners will:

  • Wipe down accessible countertops and backsplashes.
  • Clean and sanitize the kitchen sink and faucet.
  • Wipe down the exterior of cabinets and drawers, including accessible handles.
  • Clean the exterior of the refrigerator, dishwasher, oven, and microwave.
  • Clean the stovetop and accessible burner surfaces.
  • Clean the inside of the microwave.
  • Wipe down accessible small appliance exteriors, such as coffee makers and toasters.
  • Clean and polish accessible fixtures.
  • Wipe down tables and chairs.
  • Remove visible crumbs and light food residue from accessible surfaces.
  • Empty trash cans and replace liners when customer-provided liners are available.
  • Sweep and mop accessible hard floors.
  • Vacuum accessible rugs.
Bathrooms

Our cleaners will:

  • Clean and sanitize toilets, including the bowl, seat, lid, exterior, and surrounding accessible surfaces.
  • Clean bathroom sinks, countertops, and faucets.
  • Clean mirrors and accessible glass surfaces.
  • Clean showers and bathtubs, including accessible walls, fixtures, and surfaces.
  • Remove normal soap residue and light buildup.
  • Wipe down accessible cabinet and drawer exteriors.
  • Clean and polish accessible bathroom fixtures.
  • Wipe down towel bars, toilet paper holders, and other accessible fixtures.
  • Empty bathroom trash cans and replace liners when available.
  • Sweep and mop accessible bathroom floors.

*Please note: Heavy mineral deposits, extensive soap scum, mold remediation, and severely neglected bathrooms may require additional services.*

Bedrooms

Our cleaners will:

  • Dust accessible furniture surfaces, including dressers, nightstands, and desks.
  • Dust accessible lamps and decorative items.
  • Dust accessible shelves and windowsills.
  • Wipe down mirrors.
  • Lightly dust accessible bed frames and headboards.
  • Make beds using existing bedding.
  • Straighten pillows and bedding.
  • Empty trash cans and replace liners when available.
  • Vacuum accessible carpeted floors and area rugs.
  • Sweep and mop accessible hard floors.
  • Lightly dust accessible baseboards.

*Bed linen changes are not included unless specifically selected as an additional service.*

Living Rooms and Common Areas

Our cleaners will:

  • Dust accessible tables, shelves, and furniture.
  • Dust accessible television stands, entertainment centers, and electronics exteriors.
  • Wipe down accessible coffee tables and end tables.
  • Lightly dust accessible decorative items.
  • Dust accessible windowsills.
  • Clean accessible mirrors.
  • Straighten sofa cushions and throw pillows.
  • Lightly dust accessible baseboards.
  • Remove visible cobwebs within normal reach.
  • Empty trash cans and replace liners when available.
  • Vacuum accessible carpeting and area rugs.
  • Sweep and mop accessible hard floors.
Dining Areas

Our cleaners will:

  • Wipe down dining tables and accessible chair surfaces.
  • Dust accessible furniture and decorative items.
  • Clean accessible mirrors and windowsills.
  • Lightly dust accessible baseboards.
  • Vacuum accessible rugs and carpeting.
  • Sweep and mop accessible hard floors.
Hallways, Entryways, and Staircases

Our cleaners will:

  • Dust accessible ledges, railings, and banisters.
  • Spot-clean light marks from accessible light switches and door handles.
  • Remove accessible cobwebs.
  • Lightly dust accessible baseboards.
  • Clean accessible interior entry door surfaces where needed.
  • Vacuum carpeted stairs and hallways.
  • Sweep and mop accessible hard flooring.
General Cleaning Throughout the Home

The following services are included in all accessible rooms covered by the booking:

  • Dusting accessible horizontal surfaces.
  • Light dusting of baseboards.
  • Spot-cleaning light fingerprints on switches and door handles.
  • Cleaning accessible mirrors.
  • Removing reachable cobwebs.
  • Vacuuming accessible carpeting and rugs.
  • Sweeping and mopping accessible hard floors.
  • Emptying small household trash cans.
  • General straightening of pillows, cushions, and small decorative items.

All standard cleaning is performed on accessible surfaces without moving heavy furniture, disassembling fixtures, or using ladders.

Before your clean

Accessibility

Cleaners will service surfaces and areas that are reasonably accessible without moving heavy furniture or personal belongings.

Areas blocked by excessive clutter, fragile belongings, or unsafe conditions may be skipped.

Condition of the Home

Standard Cleaning is intended for homes in reasonably maintained condition.

Homes with heavy dirt, excessive grease, significant buildup, or prolonged neglect may require Deep Cleaning instead.

If the property's condition exceeds the booked scope, Turnly will contact the customer regarding available options before performing additional billable work.

Pets

For the safety and comfort of everyone involved, pets should be safely secured when necessary.

Turnly is not responsible for cleaning pet accidents or hazardous animal waste under Standard Cleaning.

Supplies and Equipment

Unless otherwise stated in the booking, Turnly's cleaning professionals will arrive with the supplies and equipment needed to perform the scheduled service.

Customers should notify Turnly in advance of delicate surfaces, special cleaning instructions, or known product restrictions.

Personal Belongings

Customers should secure valuables, important documents, fragile items, and sensitive personal belongings before the scheduled appointment.

Cleaners will not open closed cabinets, drawers, or personal storage containers unless specifically authorized as part of an approved service.

Service Limitations

Standard Cleaning focuses on routine household dirt, dust, and normal surface buildup.

Although our cleaners work carefully and thoroughly, Turnly does not guarantee the removal of permanent stains, discoloration, scratches, etching, corrosion, or damage requiring repair or restoration.

Customer Satisfaction

Turnly strives to deliver consistent, high-quality cleaning.

If a task included in your confirmed scope was missed or improperly completed, please contact Turnly through the customer portal within 24 hours of service completion.

Turnly will review the concern and, when appropriate, arrange a corrective touch-up for the affected area at no additional charge.

Additional services or cleaning tasks outside the original booking are not included in complimentary touch-ups.

Property Condition

Deep Cleaning is intended for residential properties with normal to moderate accumulated dirt, dust, grease, and household buildup.

Properties with excessive contamination, extensive clutter, severe neglect, or conditions requiring industrial cleaning may require a separate estimate.

If the property's condition exceeds the scheduled scope, Turnly will contact the customer regarding available options before performing additional billable work.

Customer Preparation

To help our cleaning professionals provide the best possible service, customers are encouraged to:

  • Remove excessive clutter from floors and surfaces.
  • Secure jewelry, cash, valuables, and sensitive documents.
  • Put away clothing, toys, and personal items.
  • Ensure running water and electricity are available.
  • Provide safe access to all scheduled cleaning areas.
  • Secure pets when necessary.
  • Inform Turnly of delicate materials, damaged fixtures, or special surface-care requirements.

Areas that cannot be accessed safely or reasonably may be skipped.

Accessibility and Safety

Our cleaning professionals will not move heavy furniture or appliances or perform tasks requiring unsafe lifting or climbing.

Cleaning is limited to areas safely accessible from floor level, including use of appropriate extension cleaning tools.

Turnly reserves the right to decline tasks that could result in injury or property damage.

Cleaning Results

Deep Cleaning is designed to remove accumulated household dirt and moderate surface buildup.

Certain stains, discoloration, corrosion, mineral deposits, worn finishes, or damaged materials may not be removable through ordinary cleaning methods.

Turnly does not guarantee restoration of damaged surfaces or removal of permanent staining.
